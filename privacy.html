Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026.

Vosiley Uploader is a personal application used only by its owner for the owner's own TikTok account.

  • Data accessed: basic profile information (display name, avatar) and an access token issued by TikTok after the owner authorizes the app.
  • Use: solely to upload and publish the owner's own videos through the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Storage: the token is stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sent to third parties, sold, or shared, and no server collects it.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services).
